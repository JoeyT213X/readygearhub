## Directory card photo pass, 27 November 2026 (`public/directory/photos/`, `src/data/card-photos.ts`)

Every listing card on the Doomsday Prepper directory and most cards on the other `/directory/*` pages now show a picture of the listed product, or for a company or service card, that company's own product, project or installation. Real photos come first: each was taken from the brand's own official product or site page (its `og:image`, JSON-LD product image or product gallery), downloaded once, white borders trimmed, and re-encoded as self-hosted WebP at 480 and 960 px (never wider than the source; no upscaling). No hotlinking. Product photos remain the property of each brand and are used to identify the product being listed.

Root cause of the blank-looking Ring and Nest covers: in `global.css` the directory-card photo rule set `mix-blend-mode: normal` with a more specific selector (`img.source-fit`) than the `multiply` rule in `refresh.css`, so white-background product photos rendered as hard white squares inside the pastel 16:10 stage. Square photos with wide white margins (Ring) and white products (Nest Cam) then read as empty boxes on a phone. Fixed site-wide by restoring `multiply` on that rule, and the Ring and Nest files were re-cut with their white margins trimmed.
