# Fill it in once. Print it. Find it when the phones are dead.

A household emergency plan only works if you can still read it when the power is out and the phones are dead. This fillable US Letter PDF gives you typed form fields for the people, numbers, medicines, shut-offs, and meeting places that Ready.gov already tells families to write down — plus a supply checklist and a documents list.

Type into the fields in a regular PDF reader, or print the pages and fill them by hand. Keep a paper copy where everyone in the house can find it, and tell at least one trusted person where it lives. The safety notes follow Ready.gov, the CDC, and FoodSafety.gov.

It is a starter binder, not a legal document vault. Leave passwords and full Social Security numbers out of it.

## What's included

- 12-page US Letter fillable PDF, with AcroForm fields you can type into and print
- Household, contacts, medical, insurance, utilities, evacuation, pets, supply checklist, and documents pages
- U.S. 911 and Poison Help (1-800-222-1222) pre-printed; all local numbers are left for you to look up
- Carbon monoxide and generator safety notes from Ready.gov and the CDC
- Personal-use license; print as many household copies as you need

## Who it's for

Households that want a printed, fillable emergency plan they can complete before a storm or outage, then find in a drawer when screens and cell service are down.
