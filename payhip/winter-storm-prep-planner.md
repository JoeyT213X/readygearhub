# A seven-day countdown for the week before the ice — not a generic to-do list.

When a Winter Storm Watch goes up, most people mean to get ready and then run out of time. This fillable US Letter planner breaks the week into a countdown: water and food, the house and the pipes, the car and cash, the kit and the contacts, then a one-room warmth plan for the outage itself.

Type into the form fields in a regular PDF reader, or print the pages and fill them by hand. The checklists and food-safety times are taken from Ready.gov, the CDC, and FoodSafety.gov — including the 20-foot generator rule, the 3-foot space-heater clearance, and the closed-fridge and freezer times.

Print it at 100% on US Letter paper and keep it with the kit.

## What's included

- 10-page US Letter fillable PDF
- Seven-day pre-storm countdown, winterization checklist, and a supplies list
- One-room warmth plan with carbon monoxide and space-heater rules
- Outage log and after-the-storm food-safety and recovery page
- Written contacts and check-in plan, plus a sources page you can keep

## Who it's for

Households in winter-storm or ice-storm country who want a short, printable plan they can start when a Watch is issued, not after the ice is already on the lines.
