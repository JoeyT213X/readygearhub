# When the well pump stops, the first problem is water — not a bigger generator.

A rural outage is different from a night in town. The tap can stop when the well pump stops, the driveway can hold you in, and the temptation to drag a generator into a barn or a back room is exactly the mistake this guide is written to prevent.

The Homestead Prep Starter Guide is a practical sequence: stored water, indoor-safe battery power, outdoor-only generators, food that does not need the fridge, heat without carbon monoxide, communications, animals, and a storm-day order of operations. Every safety number is cited to Ready.gov, the CDC, or FoodSafety.gov.

Use it as a starter plan for a farm, a cabin, or a house on a well, then write your own well, tank, and animal notes on the form pages.

## What's included

- 10-page US Letter practical guide, with lined form fields for your own property notes
- Water when the pump is dark, food-safety times during an outage, and a realistic power plan
- Generator, camp-stove, and heater rules stated the way Ready.gov and the CDC state them
- Communications, driveway and tree safety, animals, and a storm-day sequence
- A sources page so you can go back to the official guidance yourself

## Who it's for

People on a farm, a cabin, or a house with a well who need a written water and generator plan before the next ice storm, not during it.
