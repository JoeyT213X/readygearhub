GRAYL lists the UltraPress at 16.9 fl oz (500 ml) capacity and 12.5 oz (354 g) in weight, with a claimed 10-second press (3 liters per minute) and a replaceable purifier cartridge rated for 300 cycles (40 gal / 150 L), or when press time reaches about 25 seconds, or three years after first use. The company says the cartridge removes waterborne pathogens including viruses, bacteria, and protozoan cysts, and that ultra-powdered activated carbon filters particulates and adsorbs many chemicals, pesticides, herbicides, heavy metals, VOCs, flavors, and odors. GRAYL states the bottle meets or exceeds the U.S. EPA water-purifier standard for microbiological filtration and was independently tested to NSF/ANSI 42 and 53 protocols. An unopened cartridge, stored properly, has a 10-year shelf life per GRAYL. The company says the bottle is for freshwater, not salt or brackish water.
