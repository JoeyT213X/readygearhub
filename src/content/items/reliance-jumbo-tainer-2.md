Reliance lists the Jumbo-Tainer 2.0 at 7 gallons (26 L), with dual handles, a slender profile, and an easy-pour, leak-proof hideaway spigot. The official page lists dimensions of 15.3 x 8.5 x 18.0 inches and a weight of 2.9 lbs, and states the container is food safe (LFGB certified and FDA compliant, test report T32520370655SC) and PFAS- and BPA-free. Ready.gov recommends food-grade containers for water you store yourself, thoroughly cleaned and sanitized before filling, and replacing water that has not been commercially bottled every six months. Follow Reliance's care instructions and store the filled container cool and out of direct sun.
