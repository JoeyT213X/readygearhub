BLUETTI lists the AC70 at 768Wh capacity and 1,000W rated power, with 2,000W lifting power and two 120V outlets (1,000W total). The company describes a LiFePO4 battery rated for 3,000+ cycles, 7 outlets, 850W turbo charging (45 minutes to 80%), 500W solar input, a full solar charge in 2 hours under those conditions, app control over Bluetooth, 20ms UPS switchover, and dimensions of 12.4 x 8.2 x 10.1 inches. Capacity can be expanded with certain BLUETTI expansion batteries in the modes BLUETTI describes; see the official page for compatibility and current specifications.

Power stations produce no exhaust and are intended to be used indoors, unlike gas generators. Confirm the wattage of anything you plug in, especially medical devices, against BLUETTI's current output ratings.
