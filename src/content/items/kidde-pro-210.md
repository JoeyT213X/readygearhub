Kidde describes the Pro 210 as designed for commercial use to meet code compliance for common fires involving wood, paper, textiles, flammable liquids, and electrical equipment, and lists a UL rating of 2-A:10-B:C. The unit is rechargeable, per Kidde, and ships with a location sign and inspection tag so service can be documented in accordance with NFPA 10. Follow Kidde's instructions and local code for placement, inspection, and recharge. Outages raise fire risk when people use candles, space heaters, or indoor cooking they should not; an extinguisher is a complement to safe habits, not a substitute for them.
