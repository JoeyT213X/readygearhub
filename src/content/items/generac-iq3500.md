Generac's official page lists this unit as Portable Inverter Generator, 3500i | With COsense | 50ST (base model 7723, variant G0077231), also called the iQ3500. Generac lists 3,000 running watts, a 2.6-gallon fuel tank, 8.9 hours of run time at 50% load, electric start with a battery included, and a weight of 109.1 lb, with dimensions of 23.7 x 18 x 21.7 inches. The company describes COsense as technology that "Stops, Alerts, Saves" and provides protection against hazardous carbon monoxide. Features Generac lists include a PowerDial that combines start, run, and stop, a digital display with wattage, run-time remaining, status, hour meter, and fuel level, a 30A twist-lock outlet, and parallel-ready operation with a second iQ3500 (Parallel Kit #7668 sold separately). Generac's PowerRush claim is "over 50% more starting capacity" above rated output when starting electric motors; see the official page for the full wording and current specifications.

Operate this generator only outdoors, following Generac's ventilation and weather instructions. The CDC says to use generators only outside, more than 20 feet away from any windows, doors, and vents, and never inside a home or garage, even if doors and windows are open. Use a battery-powered or battery-backup carbon monoxide alarm inside the house. Do not backfeed a wall outlet; use a properly installed transfer switch or outdoor-rated cords to individual appliances.
