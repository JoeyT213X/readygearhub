Petzl lists the ACTIK CORE at 625 lumens (ANSI/PLATO FL 1) and 88 g with the CORE rechargeable battery, plus USB-C charging (cable not included). The HYBRID CONCEPT design, per Petzl, also works with three AAA/LR03 batteries (not included) and automatically adjusts output; on alkaline AAA cells Petzl rates max power at 450 lumens. Other manufacturer-stated features include IPX4 weather resistance, red lighting and a strobe, a LOCK function, and a phosphorescent reflector to help find the lamp in the dark. Follow Petzl's instructions. A headlamp leaves both hands free when you are packing a kit or walking on ice.
