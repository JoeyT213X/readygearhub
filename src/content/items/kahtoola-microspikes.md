Kahtoola describes MICROspikes as stretch-on traction for ice and packed snow, with 12 heat-treated stainless steel spikes per foot (0.41 inch / 10.4 mm, per the manufacturer) and a TPE harness that Kahtoola says retains its elasticity down to -22°F (-30°C). A tote sack is included. See Kahtoola's sizing guide for shoes versus insulated boots, inspect the device before each use, and take them off between uses, as the manufacturer instructs.
