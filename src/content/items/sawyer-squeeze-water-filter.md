Sawyer describes the Squeeze as a 0.1 micron absolute hollow-fiber filter that fits in the palm of your hand and weighs 3 ounces (Sawyer lists a kit weight of 8.19 ounces). The company says it removes 99.99999% of bacteria (including salmonella, cholera, leptospirosis, and E. coli), 99.9999% of protozoa (such as giardia and cryptosporidium), and 100% of microplastics, citing independent testing by Hydration, LLC (microbiological report S05-03). The kit, per Sawyer, includes two reusable BPA-free collapsible pouches, a cleaning plunger and coupling, gravity tubing, inline hydration-pack adapters, and a mesh storage bag. Sawyer says the filter has a removable flip-top cap and fits standard 28 mm bottle threads. See the official page for current specifications, use limits, and care instructions.
