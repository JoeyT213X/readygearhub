Reliance Controls lists the 31410CRK as a Pro/Tran 10-circuit, 30-amp generator transfer switch kit. This is the safe way to feed selected house circuits from a portable generator. Have a licensed electrician install it. Never backfeed a panel by plugging a generator into a dryer or wall outlet.
