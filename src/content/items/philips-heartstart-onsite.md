Philips describes the HeartStart OnSite as an automated external defibrillator designed so a person with little or no training can be guided by step-by-step voice commands, including pad placement and CPR, in a suspected sudden cardiac arrest. Official specifications include SMART truncated exponential biphasic waveform, nominal 150 J adult energy into a 50 ohm load (50 J with the optional Infant/Child SMART Pads cartridge for infants and children under 25 kg / 55 lbs or 0–8 years old, per Philips), daily, weekly, and monthly automatic self-tests, and a blinking green Ready light. The Ready-Pack configuration, per Philips, arrives in a carry case with the battery and adult pads installed and a spare adult pad cartridge. This is not medical advice and is not a substitute for calling 911. Get CPR and AED training, follow the device prompts and the owner's manual, and check local rules for owning and placing an AED. See Philips for current configuration, pads and battery life, and support.
