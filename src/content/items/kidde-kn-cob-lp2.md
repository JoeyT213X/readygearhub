Kidde lists the KN-COB-LP2 as a battery-operated electrochemical CO alarm with 2 AA batteries included, a Test/Reset button, a low-battery hush that can silence the chirp for up to 12 hours, and a loud 85 dB alarm at 10 feet. The company states a 10-year life and a 10-year limited warranty for current production (alarms made before 2013 are an exception; see Kidde's user guide). Follow Kidde's placement instructions. A working CO alarm is essential in winter outages, when people are tempted to use generators, grills, or heaters unsafely. Never run a generator, grill, camp stove, or charcoal indoors or in a garage.
