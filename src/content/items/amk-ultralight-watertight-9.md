Adventure Medical Kits describes the Ultralight/Watertight .9 as a kit for multi-sport trips of 1–4 days with 1–4 people. The official page lists two-stage waterproofing: a 100% waterproof DryFlex inner bag and a water-resistant outer bag with a durable water-repellent finish. AMK says the kit includes supplies for common outdoor injuries and emergency trauma care for bleeding, fractures, and sprains, including a trauma pad, dressings, and wraps.
