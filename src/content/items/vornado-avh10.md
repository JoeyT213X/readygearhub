Vornado describes the AVH10 as a whole-room electric heater that uses the company's Vortex airflow to circulate warm air, with two heat settings plus fan-only mode, automatic climate control, push-button controls and a digital display, a cool-touch case, an integrated cord wrap, and an automatic safety shutoff system. A 5-year satisfaction guarantee is listed on the official page. Read and follow every clearance and safety instruction in Vornado's owner's guide.

This heater needs household electricity or a correctly sized outdoor generator. It will not run during a grid-down outage on its own. Keep space heaters away from bedding, curtains, and furniture, never leave one unattended, and do not use a generator, grill, camp stove, or outdoor-rated heater indoors to replace it. Use a working smoke alarm and, if any fuel-burning equipment is in use on the property, a working carbon monoxide alarm.
