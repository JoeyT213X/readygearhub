3M describes the Indoor Window Insulator Kit (Two Pack, 2120W-6) as heat-shrink window film with Scotch double-sided mounting tape, sized to fit two 3-by-5-foot indoor windows. The company says the kits keep cold drafts out and warm air in, reduce condensation, and prevent frost buildup, and that the film is meant to stay up all season. Follow 3M's installation instructions and the safety data sheet on the product page. This does not replace a working heat source, but sealing drafts is one of the simplest ways to help a single room stay warmer in a winter outage.
