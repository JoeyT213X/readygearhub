Midland describes the WR400 Deluxe as a Public Alert certified tabletop radio that can be programmed with up to 25 county or province S.A.M.E. codes and offers siren, voice, or visual LED alerts, plus AM/FM and an alarm clock. Midland lists emergency power backup that requires 4 AA batteries (not included) and a USB charging port for a phone during outages. An AC adapter and owner's manual are included. Install the backup batteries before a storm, and program the S.A.M.E. codes for your counties using the National Weather Service list Midland points to.
