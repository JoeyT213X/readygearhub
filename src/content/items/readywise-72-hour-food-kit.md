ReadyWise lists the 72 Hour Food Kit with 30 servings and a 25-year shelf life, and describes the contents as one pouch of Cheesy Macaroni (6 servings), one pouch of Teriyaki Rice (6 servings), one pouch of Brown Sugar and Maple Multi-Grain Cereal (6 servings), and one pouch of whey milk (12 servings). Additional official figures are 5,460 total calories and three days of food at 1,820 calories per day. See the official page for current preparation directions, allergens, and storage guidance. This is a short-term kit; pair it with stored water and a longer food reserve if you expect a multi-day or two-week stay at home.
