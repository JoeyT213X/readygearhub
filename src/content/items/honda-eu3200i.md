Honda lists the EU3200i at 3200W maximum / 2600W rated (120V), with a Honda GX130 130cc fuel-injected engine, inverter output, Eco-Throttle, and Bluetooth connectivity with the Honda My Generator app. The company states a dry weight of 59.1 lb, a 1.2-gallon tank, run time of 3.3 hours at rated load and 8.6 hours at 1/4 load, and a noise level of 58 dB(A) at rated load and 54 dB(A) at 1/4 load. Receptacles, per Honda, include a 30A 125V locking plug and a 20A 125V duplex. Honda's CO-MINDER system is designed to automatically shut the generator down if detected carbon monoxide near the unit reaches unsafe levels, per Honda. Full model names are EU3200IAN (49-state) and EU3200IAC (50-state).

Operate this generator only outdoors, following Honda's ventilation and weather instructions. The CDC says to use generators only outside, more than 20 feet away from any windows, doors, and vents, and never inside a home or garage, even if doors and windows are open. Use a battery-powered or battery-backup carbon monoxide alarm inside the house. Do not backfeed a wall outlet; use a properly installed transfer switch or outdoor-rated cords to individual appliances.
