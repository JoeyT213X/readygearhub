Augason Farms lists this kit as a 30-day, 1-person emergency food supply with 194 servings, 1,290 calories per day, and 36 grams of protein per day. The company says the meals are shelf-stable, require no refrigeration, and are prepared by adding water and cooking. Official examples of included varieties include brown sugar oatmeal, buttermilk pancake and waffle mix, strawberry-flavored creamy wheat, creamy rice and vegetables, and cheesy broccoli rice; see the current product page for the full lineup, allergens, and pouch counts. Individual foods on the official page list shelf-life figures of up to 25 or 30 years depending on the item. Store the pail in a cool, dry place as the manufacturer directs, and plan a way to boil or heat water that is safe for indoor or outdoor use.
