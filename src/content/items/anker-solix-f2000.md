Anker lists the SOLIX F2000 (PowerHouse 767) at 2,048Wh capacity and 2,400W AC output, with LiFePO4 batteries the company rates for up to 3,000 charge cycles. The official page also describes a maximum 1,000W solar input and the option to add an expansion battery for 4,096Wh of total capacity. Anker states the unit can run high-demand devices at the same time up to 3,600W; see the official page for how that feature is defined and for current specifications. Do not treat marketing runtime examples as a guarantee for your own appliances.

Energy reserves produce no exhaust and are intended to be used indoors, unlike gas generators. Confirm the wattage of anything you plug in against Anker's current ratings.
