Reliance Outdoors lists the original Aqua-Tainer in 4G/15L (#9405-03R) and 7G/26L (#9410-03R). The manufacturer describes a rugged, thick-walled, stackable-when-empty design, a hideaway spigot that reverses for storage, a screw-on vent cap, and a molded handle. Reliance states the container is LFGB certified and FDA compliant, and PFAS- and BPA-free. The 7-gallon model, per Reliance, measures 11.50 by 11.25 by 15.75 inches and weighs 2 lbs empty; the 4-gallon model is the same footprint and 10.75 inches tall at 1.5 lbs. Ready.gov's common guideline is about 1 gallon of water per person per day; size the container to your household and store it clean and out of the sun.
