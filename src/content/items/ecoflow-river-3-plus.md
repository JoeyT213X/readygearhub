EcoFlow lists the RIVER 3 Plus at 286Wh capacity and 600W output, with a LiFePO4 battery the company says holds 80% capacity for over 10 years of use, UPS auto-switch under 10 milliseconds, and three AC outlets with UPS protection. EcoFlow says capacity can expand to 858Wh with the official Extra Battery, and that the unit can recharge from AC, solar, a car, or a generator. Runtime examples on the official page include a 3W Wi-Fi router for up to 35 hours and a 600W desktop for at least 21 minutes; those figures are EcoFlow's, not independent tests. See the official page for current specifications and charging options.

Power stations produce no exhaust and are intended to be used indoors, unlike gas generators. Confirm device wattage before you rely on this unit during an outage.
