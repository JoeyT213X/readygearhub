Anker lists the Prime Power Bank (model A110B) at 20,100 mAh capacity, 220W maximum total output, 140W maximum USB-C output, and 100W maximum USB-C input, in a 1.12 lb (510 g) body measuring 1.73 by 1.99 by 5.79 inches. Anker also describes PowerIQ 4.0 power distribution and ActiveShield 4.0 temperature monitoring. See Anker's page for port layout and charging-cable requirements (the company notes a 5A cable and 100W-or-higher charger to reach 100W input). This is a device charger, not a power station for appliances or space heaters. Charge it fully before a forecast storm.
