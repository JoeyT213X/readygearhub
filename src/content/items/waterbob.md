The manufacturer describes the waterBOB as a heavy-duty, 10 mil food-grade LLDPE bathtub liner that holds up to 100 gallons (most standard tubs hold about 60–70 gallons, per the company) and fills in about 20 to 30 minutes. A siphon pump is included. The company says water stays fresh for up to 16 weeks depending on humidity and temperature, that the liner is intended for one-time use, and that it should not be filled over the edge of the tub or left in direct sunlight. Fill it while the water is still on, before a storm, and follow the printed instructions.
