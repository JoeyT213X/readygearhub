Honda lists the EU2200i at 2200W maximum / 1800W rated (120V), with a Honda GXR120 121cc engine, inverter output, Eco-Throttle, and optional parallel operation with a second identical unit. The company states a dry weight of 47.4 lb, a 0.95-gallon tank, run time of 3.2 hours at rated load and 8.1 hours at 1/4 load, and a noise level of 57 dB(A) at rated load and 48 dB(A) at 1/4 load. Honda's CO-MINDER system continuously monitors carbon monoxide and automatically shuts the generator down if detected CO near the unit becomes too high, per Honda.

Operate this generator only outdoors, following Honda's ventilation instructions. The CDC says to keep generators at least 20 feet from the home, never in a garage, basement, or near open windows. Always use a working carbon monoxide alarm inside the house. Do not backfeed a wall outlet; use a properly installed transfer switch or outdoor-rated cords to individual appliances.
