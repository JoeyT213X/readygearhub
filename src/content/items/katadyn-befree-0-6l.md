Katadyn lists the BeFree 0.6L as a hollow-fiber filter that removes bacteria, cysts, and sediment at a 0.1 micron (0.0001 mm) pore size, and states it is for clear water only. Official specifications include a weight of 59 grams, output up to 2 liters per minute, and a treated quantity of up to 1,000 liters depending on water quality. Katadyn says the EZ-Clean Membrane can be cleaned by shaking or swishing it in water, with no backflushing tools required, and that the flask is 100% PVC- and BPA-free. This is a filter, not a virus purifier; see Katadyn's page for what it does and does not remove, and follow the official care instructions.
