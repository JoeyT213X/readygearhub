Adventure Medical Kits describes this Trauma Pak as a compact, waterproof kit with supplies to help control bleeding until medical professionals arrive. The official page lists QuikClot Z-fold gauze (2 ft), a 5 x 9 inch trauma pad, sterile gauze, a conforming bandage, a triangular bandage, gloves, alcohol swabs, and a short roll of duct tape, in a resealable DryFlex bag, at a listed weight of 4.4 oz. AMK also says the pack includes a one-page trauma instruction sheet. This is not a substitute for calling 911 or for first-aid training (for example, Red Cross or Stop the Bleed). See the official page for current contents and any use warnings, and follow the manufacturer's directions.
