TETON Sports lists the Celsius Regular as an oversized rectangular bag with SuperLoft Elite fill, a polyester taffeta shell, a mummy-style hood, a two-way zipper, draft tubes, and a compression sack. The company is clear that 0°F is a survival temperature rating — the coldest temperature the bag is designed to keep you alive in, not necessarily comfortable — and suggests adding 20–30 degrees for a comfort estimate (about 25°F to 30°F, per TETON). Manufacturer specs include an 80-by-33-inch in-use size, 67-inch girth at shoulder, hip, and foot, a fit up to 5'8", and a 5 lb item weight. Use a pad underneath in a cold house, and do not treat a survival rating as a promise of comfortable sleep at 0°F.
