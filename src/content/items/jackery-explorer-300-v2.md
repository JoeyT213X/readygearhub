Jackery lists the Explorer 300 v2 at 288Wh capacity and 300W output (600W surge), with dual AC outlets, 100W USB-C fast charging, and a LiFePO4 battery the company rates at 4,000+ cycles. Jackery also lists an 8.1 lb (3.7 kg) weight, an industrial-grade UPS with a response of 0.01 seconds or less, recharge to 80% in 1 hour via wall or solar (per Jackery), and a stated operating range of –15°C to 45°C. See Jackery's page for current ports and charging options. A compact energy reserve like this is for lights, phones, and similar loads, not space heaters.
