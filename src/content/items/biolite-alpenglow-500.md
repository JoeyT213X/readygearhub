BioLite lists the AlpenGlow 500 at 500 lumens on high and 5 lumens on low, with a stated burn time of 5 hours on high and up to 200 hours on low, plus about 1 hour of reserve power. The manufacturer lists a 6400 mAh (23.6 Wh) li-ion battery, IPX4 water resistance, a weight of 13.4 oz (380 g), cool and warm white modes, a red night mode, and USB-A output (5V / 2.4A) for charging phones. Charging is via micro USB, in about 3 hours per BioLite, and a cable is included. BioLite notes it as useful for home emergencies. Bring it inside in freezing temperatures to protect the battery, as the company recommends.
