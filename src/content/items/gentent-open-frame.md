GenTent lists this as a universal open-frame generator tent. CPSC notes that some maker instructions may include an NFPA-rated noncombustible generator tent for wet weather. A canopy does not change the 20-foot rule. Do not run a generator on a porch, in a carport, or under a house overhang.
