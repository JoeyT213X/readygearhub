Jackery lists the SolarSaga 200W at a peak power of 200W ±5% (standard test conditions), with monocrystalline cells the company rates at up to 25% conversion efficiency and a bifacial design that can collect reflected light from the rear. The official page lists an IP68 rating, a weight of 14.33 lbs ± 0.66 lbs, folded dimensions of 23.62 x 23.50 x 1.57 in, unfolded dimensions of 89.76 x 23.50 x 0.98 in, an operating range of -4 to 149°F, and built-in USB-A (5V, 2.4A) and USB-C (5V, 3A) outputs. The kit, per Jackery, includes a 3-meter multi-function cable and a DC8020 to DC7909 adapter. Confirm connector compatibility with your power station on Jackery's page. Actual output varies with sun, angle, and weather.
