Coleman lists this Classic two-burner stove at 20,000 total BTUs, with two independently adjustable burners, WindBlock panels, PerfectFlow pressure regulation, and a removable chrome-plated grate. The company says it fits a 12-inch and a 10-inch pan at the same time, lasts up to 1 hour with both burners on high on one 16.4-oz propane cylinder (sold separately), and is intended for camping, hunting, tailgating, and other outdoor occasions. Model 5430 / SKU 2223621, per Coleman.

Never use this stove indoors, in a garage, in a tent, or near open windows. Camp stoves produce carbon monoxide, which can kill quickly in enclosed spaces. Cook only outside, keep a working CO alarm in the home, and follow Coleman's lighting and clearance instructions.
