SAM Medical describes the SAM Splint as a lightweight, reusable, radiolucent, cleanable foam-and-aluminum splint that becomes supportive when bent into a curve and can be cut to size with ordinary scissors. The official store lists sizes including a finger splint, 9-inch, 18-inch, 36-inch (fold or roll), and 36-inch XL, in more than one color. Standard width is listed as 4.25 inches, with XL at 5.5 inches. This is not medical advice and is not a substitute for professional care. Use it only as trained, and seek emergency medical help for suspected fractures.
