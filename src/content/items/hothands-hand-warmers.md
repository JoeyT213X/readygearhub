Kobayashi Americas, the manufacturer, describes HotHands Hand Warmers as rectangular, single-use, air-activated heat packs that you shake to start. The company says they provide up to 10 hours of continuous warmth for the hands and lists them as odorless, disposable, and generally TSA-allowed, with the caveat that screening rules can vary. Follow the instructions on the package, and do not apply warmers directly to bare skin if sensation to heat is reduced, which Kobayashi notes can be an issue for some people, including young children, people with diabetes, and older adults.
