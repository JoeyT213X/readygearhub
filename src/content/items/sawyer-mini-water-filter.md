Sawyer describes the second-generation MINI as a 0.1 micron absolute hollow-fiber filter that fits in the palm of your hand, with a field weight of just 2 ounces. The company says it removes 99.99999% of bacteria (including salmonella, cholera, and E. coli), 99.9999% of protozoa (such as giardia and cryptosporidium), and 100% of microplastics, and lists longevity of up to 100,000 gallons. The kit, per Sawyer, includes the filter, a 16 oz pouch, a backwash syringe, and a straw. Sawyer warns not to attach the filter to a pressurized spigot, and says a wetted filter should be kept from freezing (store it next to your body in freezing weather) or replaced if you suspect it froze.
