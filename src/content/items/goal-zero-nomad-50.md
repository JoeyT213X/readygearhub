Goal Zero lists the Nomad 50 at 50W rated power, with four monocrystalline panels in a protective case, a weight of 6.85 lbs, unfolded dimensions of 17 x 53 x 1.5 in, and folded dimensions of 17 x 11.25 x 2.5 in. Ports, per Goal Zero, include a 14–21.5V 8mm solar port at up to 3.3A (50W max) and a USB-A port at 5V, up to 2.4A (12W max). The company says Nomad 50 panels can be chained up to 150W, and lists an open-circuit voltage of 21.5V and a maximum power-point voltage of 18.0V. Goal Zero notes that the panel itself does not store energy and that charge times vary with sun, angle, and conditions. Keep the energy reserve you are charging protected from rain, as Goal Zero advises.
