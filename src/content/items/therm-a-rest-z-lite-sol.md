On the official Cascade Designs page, Therm-a-Rest lists the Z Lite SOL as a molded closed-cell foam pad with a folding, accordion-style design. The company states an R-value of 2.0 and says the ThermaCapture surface should be used reflective side up; Therm-a-Rest attributes about 15% more warmth to that coating plus heat-trapping dimples. Regular size, per the manufacturer, is 20 by 72 by 0.75 inches and 14 ounces, and it is made in the USA. A pad like this is useful in a winter outage because a lot of body heat is lost to a cold floor.
