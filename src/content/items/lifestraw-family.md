LifeStraw lists the Family as a survival water purifier that uses a 0.02 micron membrane ultrafilter. The company says it removes 99.999999% of bacteria, 99.999% of viruses, 99.999% of parasites, and 99.999% of microplastics, dirt, sand, and cloudiness, and that it meets NSF/ANSI P231 standards for reduction of viruses, bacteria, and parasites. Official specifications include a maximum flow rate of 3.1 gal/hr (12 L/hr), a lifespan of up to 4,755 gal (18,000 L), a weight of 1.17 lbs, and BPA-free materials. LifeStraw's official Q&A states the Family holds 2 liters. The company notes this product does not remove chlorine or fluoride. See the official page and user manual for setup, cleaning, and storage.
