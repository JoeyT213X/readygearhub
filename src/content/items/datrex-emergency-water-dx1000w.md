Datrex lists item DX1000W as purified emergency drinking water in polymer-foil pouches, sold by the case: 64 pouches, 125 ml (4.227 fl oz) each, for 8 liters total. Official figures include a pouch weight of 4.66 oz (132 grams), pouch dimensions of 6.0 x 4.0 x 0.5 in, a case weight of 20.5 lbs, case dimensions of 13.25 x 9.75 x 6.75 inches, and a 5-year shelf life from the date of manufacture. Datrex says the product is designed for land and sea use when normal water supplies are unavailable, and that it meets U.S. Coast Guard requirements in ISO 18813:2006 in lieu of 46 CFR 160.054 and 160.041. See the official page for current packaging and storage guidance. This is drinking water for emergencies; it does not replace the larger stored supply Ready.gov recommends for home sanitation as well as drinking.
