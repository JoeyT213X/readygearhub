UCO lists the Stormproof Match Kit with 25 stormproof matches, a waterproof screw-top case that floats and can hold up to 40 matches, and extra strikers. The company says the matches are windproof and waterproof, can relight after submersion, and burn for up to 15 seconds; match length is 2.75 in (7 cm) and kit weight is 1.2 oz (34 g), per UCO. Follow the printed safety instructions: strike away from the body, keep matches away from children, and fully extinguish used matches in water. These are for lighting fuel outdoors or in devices rated for the setting you are in — not for indoor fire-starting during an outage.
