GRAYL describes the 24oz GeoPress as a Fill. Press. Drink. purifier with a 24 oz (710 ml) capacity and a weight of 15.9 oz, per the company. GRAYL says it removes waterborne pathogens (the company lists 99.99% of viruses, 99.9999% of bacteria, and 99.9% of protozoan cysts) and that the cartridge uses electroadsorption and ultra-powdered activated carbon. The manufacturer also says the cartridge is rated for 350 cycles (65 gal / 250 L) and that the bottle meets or exceeds the U.S. EPA Water Purifier standard for microbiological filtration. Follow GRAYL's instructions for cartridge replacement and use.
